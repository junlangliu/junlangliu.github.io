01 / RESEARCH

Predictive biology
across scales.

How do molecular motions give rise to cellular behavior, and how can we learn the rules well enough to predict and change it?

I

Protein dynamics
& regulation

I investigate how conformational dynamics govern protein recognition, folding, and function. My work on GroEL/S and DHFR connects molecular motions to chaperone engagement and antibiotic resistance.

I also study how changes in the surrounding water network reshape protein conformations, linking molecular mechanisms to thermodynamic principles.

Chaperones & drug resistance ↗
II

Cellular organization
& multiscale models

I study how interactions between biomolecules produce collective behavior, from enzyme clusters that accelerate metabolic reactions to the assembly and material states of biomolecular condensates.

This work motivates my interest in connecting molecular models to predictive descriptions of cellular organization and function.

Enzyme organization & metabolism ↗
III

Statistical inference
& AI for science

I am interested in combining statistical inference, physical models, and machine learning to extract mechanisms from biological data and guide protein design.

My collaborative work on VibeProteinBench evaluates language-based protein-design systems. Looking ahead, I want to connect such tools with experiments and models of cellular behavior.

AI for protein design ↗
